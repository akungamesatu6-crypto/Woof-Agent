How it works

Three steps to your first dog

01

Get an Agent ID

Minting is agent-only. You need a SPACE ID Agent ID (an .bnb name with an Agent ID) in the same wallet you mint from. Check yours in the mint panel below.

02

Mint — first one free

Connect your wallet, claim your FREE first card, then mint up to 19 more. Every mint draws a random card from the deck — you never know which dog you get until it is yours. Free claims are limited to the first 1,000 overall: once they are gone, every mint is paid.

03

Earn & trade

Every paid mint earns 1,200 $WOOF. List cards on the Woof Agent marketplace — the seller keeps 95%, and a 5% royalty supports the project.

Mint is done by your Binance Agentic Wallet agent, or directly from your wallet. The contract enforces everything on-chain: Agent ID gate, wallet limit of 20, and the price ladder. No allowlist — any Agent ID holder can mint.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Woof Agent?

Woof Agent is a 1,111-supply NFT collection of 24 original dog cards on BNB Chain. Each mint draws a random card from the deck. It is built for Binance Agentic Wallet users — minting requires a SPACE ID Agent ID.

How much does it cost to mint?

Your first mint is FREE. Paid mints cost 0.00035 BNB for mints #2–10 and 0.00065 BNB for mints #11–20. Maximum 20 mints per wallet, so the most one wallet ever pays is 0.00965 BNB.

Do I need an Agent ID?

Yes. Both the free mint and paid mints are agent-only: the contract checks that your wallet holds a SPACE ID Agent ID. Use the checker in the mint panel to verify any address before you mint.

Is the mint random?

Yes. The contract draws from one shuffled deck (swap-and-pop), shared by free and paid mints, so every remaining card has an equal chance on every mint — a free mint can pull any card, including The Alpha. There is no way to pick a specific card, and no mint is reserved.

How long is the free mint open?

Until the first 1,000 free claims are used. Each Agent ID wallet gets 1 free mint. Once 1,000 wallets have claimed, the free mint closes automatically — no owner action, no announcement — and every mint after that is paid. Paid mints keep drawing from the same deck until all 1,111 are minted.

How do I earn $WOOF?

Every paid mint accrues a flat 1,200 $WOOF. Free mints accrue 0. Claim your $WOOF on-chain any time. The most one wallet can earn is 22,800 $WOOF (19 paid mints).

What is the marketplace floor rule?

Listings must be priced at 80% or more of the card's reference price: Common 0.002, Rare 0.005, Epic 0.012, Legendary 0.03, Mythic 0.1 BNB. The seller keeps 95% of the sale; 5% is the project royalty. Listing and cancelling each cost 0.00025 BNB.

When does mint open?

The contracts are deployed. Mint is not open yet — the opening time will be shown on this page in UTC once set.

Is there a roadmap or promise of returns?

No. Woof Agent is a collectible. Nothing on this page is a promise of profit, price, or future value. Buy a card because you like the dog.
