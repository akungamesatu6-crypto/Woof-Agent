How listing works

1. Connect your wallet and approve the marketplace to move your card (one-time approval per card).
2. List with a price at or above the floor — listing costs 0.00025 BNB.
3. Your card sits in escrow. Anyone can buy it at the listed price; the BNB splits 95% to you and 5% royalty automatically.
4. Change your price any time for free, or cancel (0.00025 BNB) to get your card back.
